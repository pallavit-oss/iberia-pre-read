The Role

What the role actually involves.

The role is broken down into four parts. Here's what each one actually involves.

01 — Account Management
Own and grow existing partner relationships
Spain and Portugal have a strong base of experience partners already live on Headout. You'll own those relationships, grow their performance, and help them get more out of the platform.
02 — New Partner Acquisition
Find, pitch, and sign new supply partners
Attractions, tours, theatre, cruises, nightlife, if it's an experience worth having in Spain or Portugal, we want it on Headout. You'll find it, pitch it, and bring it on board.
03 — Internal Delivery
The deal isn't done when it's signed.
You'll work with Product, Operations, Legal, Finance, and Marketing to deliver on what was promised, and be the person partners trust to follow through.
04 — Revenue Optimisation
Use data to improve partner outcomes
Once a partner is live, the focus shifts to performance. You'll look at pricing, availability, and conversion data to find which ones are underperforming and fix it.

The Opportunity

Be part of a strong market and team.

Headout is a curated experiences marketplace covering 200 cities, with over 6,000 experiences and growing fast across Europe, APAC, the Middle East and North Africa, the US, and Latin America. Europe is our biggest geography, generating around 70% of overall revenue, and within Europe, markets like France, the UK and Spain lead the way.

Madrid and Barcelona are among the most visited cities in Europe, and Lisbon isn't far behind — together home to some of Headout's anchor attractions, from Park Güell and the Sagrada Família to the Alhambra and Pena Palace. It's a market with deep, established demand and a long runway: a lot still to bring online across Spain and Portugal, and plenty of room to grow what's already live.

This role builds on that. You'll own a high-potential portfolio of Iberia's attractions, culture, and entertainment partners, working closely with Diego Valera (GM, Iberia & South America) and the wider Iberia team, and be expected to shape the next wave of categories and experiences across Spain and Portugal. Strong performance here typically opens the door to broader scope: new venues, new categories, and eventually more responsibility, as the Iberia and Europe business continues to grow.
